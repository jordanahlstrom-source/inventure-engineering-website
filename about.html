My background

From medical devices to aerospace manufacturing systems.

I've always enjoyed understanding problems and finding creative solutions. That curiosity led me to earn both a Bachelor's and Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering and eventually work on projects ranging from medical devices to large-scale aerospace manufacturing systems.

Throughout my career I've managed complex engineering projects worth millions of dollars, designed custom manufacturing equipment, collaborated with multidisciplinary teams, and solved challenging technical problems for industry-leading organizations.

Today I bring that same level of engineering expertise to entrepreneurs, inventors, and growing businesses who need practical product development support.

Why I started Inventure

I get excited about creating something from scratch.

I started Inventure Engineering because I enjoy helping people bring ideas to life. I get excited about creating something from scratch and coming up with new ideas.

Many inventors and small businesses have great ideas but don't know where to start or can't justify hiring a full-time engineer. I wanted to create a company that provides professional engineering services in a more approachable, collaborative, and affordable way.

Whether it's a simple fixture, a consumer product, or a complex mechanical assembly, I enjoy solving problems that turn concepts into manufacturable products.
