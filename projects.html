Custom machineDemo video

Pillow Impact Machine

IDFL Laboratories

Designed and built a custom testing machine to measure feather leakage from pillow fabrics. The machine had to impact the pillow twice as fast as it retracts to accurately simulate a head hitting the pillow — a real mechanical challenge to overcome. I produced full manufacturing drawings and delivered a machine that improved testing productivity by 300%.

Custom part

Custom Floor Outlet Mounts

A contractor had installed the wrong size outlet box for floor-mounted outlets. I designed a custom mounting solution plus a matching cover to correctly and cleanly house the outlets — turning a costly rework into a simple, printable fix.

Jenga Box & Stacker

A new and improved storage box for Jenga-style stacking games. The design lets you unstack the blocks just by opening the box — instead of dumping them out and restacking by hand. A small idea, engineered into a genuinely satisfying mechanism.

Lincoln Speaker Cover

Classic car restoration

Enclosure design

DIN Rail Electrical Board Case

Designed an enclosure for an electrical board that clips directly onto a DIN rail — for fast, clean installation into an electrical panel. Integrated venting and a positive-locking clip kept the assembly simple in the field.
